What guides Tanu

Publishing infrastructure with a point of view.

The goal is not another feed. It is a calm, durable place where writers can publish and readers can settle into a story.

01

Ownership before reach

A Tanu library should be deployable and controlled by the people who rely on it, with clear exports instead of lock-in.

02

Reading without noise

Accessible typography, useful navigation, and reader comfort come first. Engagement mechanics should support a story, never interrupt it.

03

Formats that travel

Authors should be able to write in familiar source formats and publish to the web, EPUB, and other durable outputs.

One source, many forms

A practical route forward

Rebuilding in deliberate chapters.

Tanu is early. The roadmap favours a trustworthy core over a sprawling feature list, and each phase should leave behind something testable and useful.

Chapter oneNow

A dependable foundation

  • Supported long-term stack
  • Reproducible local and container setup
  • Automated quality and security checks
  • Clear architecture and contribution docs
Chapter twoNext

Stories and libraries

  • Accounts, roles, and library membership
  • Books, chapters, revisions, and metadata
  • A focused authoring and publishing flow
  • Accessible public reading pages
Chapter threeLater

Portability and community

  • EPUB and print-friendly exports
  • Bookmarks, tags, comments, and follows
  • Import, backup, and migration tools
  • Federation research without premature promises

An honest status note
